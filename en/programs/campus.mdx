## What you get

- Once approved, you receive **$200 in credits**, which can be applied directly to model API calls on the Poixe platform.
- Access to **[all models](/en/docs/models-pricing/models)** available on the platform. Choose different models as needed for course demos, research validation, or project development.
- Use the platform's **[4 mainstream API protocols](/en/api-reference/introduction/protocols)**. Integrate quickly with standard chat interfaces, and also cover the call patterns of tool clients such as **[Claude Code](/en/use-cases/code/claude-code)**.
- Connect to most open-source projects and clients on the market. As long as a project supports a custom API endpoint, it is easy to connect to Poixe.

## Eligibility (summary)

We mainly check four things:

1. **Genuine organization**: it truly comes from a university setting (course/lab/club/research group, etc.)
2. **Clear purpose**: used for teaching, research, course projects, club activities, or related demos
3. **Verifiable information**: provide basic school/organization details and contact information (a university email is recommended)
4. **Account registered**: register a Poixe account in advance and enter that account's email on the application form; we will credit the grant to that account and notify you of the review result by that email

<Callout type="warning" icon="info-circle">
  To prevent abuse: falsified information, bulk registration, and resale/top-up-on-behalf/gray-market use will be rejected outright and may result in account restrictions.
</Callout>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can the credits be withdrawn?">
    No. Credits can only be used to offset API usage on the Poixe platform.
  </Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can multiple people share it?">
  We recommend that the account be used and managed only by a single designated owner within the organization to avoid abuse risk. API Keys
  you create can be used by multiple people, with no usage restriction.
</Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if my application is rejected?">
    This is usually due to insufficient information or an unclear purpose. You can add supporting materials and reapply, or contact the support email to explain your situation.

    Contact us by email: support@poixe.com

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
