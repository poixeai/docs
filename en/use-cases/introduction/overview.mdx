Poixe AI provides a unified, standardized API protocol that lets you integrate a wide range of popular AI applications into your workflow or product.  

You usually need just two things: `base_url` and `api_key`. Enter them in the application's "API configuration" and you can start chatting or making calls right away.

This section covers several common use cases and integration methods to help you get started quickly.
